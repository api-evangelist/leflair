Leflair is a Vietnamese online retail brand selling discounted authentic international brands across fashion and accessories, beauty and health, home and lifestyle, and children's products. Its own terms of service describe it as Vietnam's first members-only flash-sale platform, running daily promotional events on branded goods.

The live storefront at [leflair.com](https://leflair.com/) is operated by Cong ty TNHH Sopa Technology of Ho Chi Minh City on the Haravan commerce platform, alongside a Vietnamese-language editorial blog and published terms, privacy, payment-security, shipping, and tax policy pages.

Backed by: 500-global

## API surface

Leflair is a consumer retail brand and publishes **no public API**. As of the 2026-07-19 enrichment pass there is no developer portal, no OpenAPI or AsyncAPI definition, no SDK or CLI, no MCP server, and no `/.well-known/` discovery documents. The historical `leflair.vn` domain no longer serves the storefront, and `api.leflair.vn` does not accept connections.
